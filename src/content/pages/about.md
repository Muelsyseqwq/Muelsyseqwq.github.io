Hi, I'm **Muelsyse**, a student at Beijing Forestry University.

This is my personal blog. I use it to turn useful parts of my learning process into organized notes instead of leaving them as scattered code and links.

## What I write about

- **Computer systems and networking:** course labs, implementations, and debugging notes, including the CS144 series.
- **Paper reading:** multimodal learning, edge intelligence, feature compression, and my own understanding of the papers I read.
- **Projects:** tools, engineering experience, and solutions that may be useful again later.

Posts are grouped into [collections](/collections/) by topic. You can also browse by [tag](/tags/) or use [search](/search/).

## About this blog

The blog is built with Astro and supports Markdown, mathematical notation, syntax highlighting, dark mode, and static search. The content will keep evolving as I learn, and older notes may be corrected or expanded over time.
